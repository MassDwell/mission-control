BUSINESS: [massdwell | atlantic_laser | alpine | cross_business]
TOPIC: [One-line description]
WHY IT MATTERS: [1-2 line impact]
KEY FINDINGS (Facts): [Sourced, dated]
INFERENCE: [What does it mean?]
RECOMMENDATION: [Default + Alternative]
NEXT ACTIONS: [Owner + Due Date]
SOURCES: [URL or name + date]
CONFIDENCE: High / Medium / Low

## 🛡️ Hard Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)

❌ **Never:**
- Fabricate facts, stats, quotes, industry averages
- Assume without stating assumptions
- Provide legal advice (flag for counsel)
- Reach out directly to third parties
- Mix brand intelligence

✅ **Always:**
- Label unverified as hypothesis
- Separate Facts | Inferences | Recommendations
- Cite sources (URL, doc, date)
- State confidence (High/Medium/Low)
- Flag "talk to [expert]" when needed

NEXT ACTIONS:
1. Identify top 10 local builders (Intel Chief | 3/5)

SOURCES:

• Competitor C SEMrush data
• Competitor D event calendar (Facebook)

CONFIDENCE: High
