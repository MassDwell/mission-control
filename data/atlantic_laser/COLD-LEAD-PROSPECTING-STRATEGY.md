# Atlantic Laser Cold Lead Prospecting Strategy

**Status:** ✅ LIVE  
**Date:** March 3, 2026  
**Lead Type:** COLD (database import, zero prior engagement)

---

## What Makes This Different

**Alpine leads are COLD:**
- Imported from fabrication/welding shop database
- No prior awareness of Atlantic Laser
- No prior interest signal
- Unsolicited outreach

**This means:**
- Lower expected reply rates (3-8% vs 15-25% for warm leads)
- Longer qualification cycle (14-21 days vs 3-7 days)
- Need stronger value props + social proof
- Need multiple touchpoints (5-7 vs 2-3)
- Must lead with ROI, not features

---

## Email Templates for Cold Leads

### Template #01: Initial Contact (COLD)

**Purpose:** Grab attention with specific ROI + establish relevance

**Opens with:** Problem + specific savings (not features)

```
Subject: Reduce welding labor costs by 40% — {{company_name}}

Before/After financial comparison:
- Before: Arc welding, $180 labor per job
- After: MA1 laser, $25 labor per job
- Savings: $155 per job = $37K/year at 20 jobs/month

Asks: "Which of these applies? High-volume production? Precision work? Labor shortage?"

Closes: Offer to send case study + reference checks (social proof)
```

**Key differences from warm leads:**
- ❌ NOT "I noticed you visited our site..." (they didn't)
- ✅ YES "Here's your specific ROI..." (prove value immediately)
- ❌ NOT "Open to a conversation?" (too soft)
- ✅ YES "Worth 10 minutes?" (direct, assumes value delivered)

---

### Template #02: Follow-up Discovery (COLD)

**Purpose:** Extract BANT + get to case study/proof quickly

**Asks:**
1. Current welding method (relevant to ROI calculation)
2. Volume (helps tailor numbers)
3. Main pain (find the hook)
4. Timeline (urgency)
5. Decision maker (authority)

**Closes with:** Promise to deliver personalized ROI + case study + references (again, social proof)

## Expected Performance

### Reply Rate: 3-8% (vs 15-25% warm)

**Why so low?**
- No prior awareness (takes longer to even notice email)
- No intent signal (might not care about laser welding)
- Skepticism of unsolicited pitch
- Spam filter risks

**Implication:** Need MORE outreach volume to get same conversation count

### Qualification Cycle: 14-21 days (vs 3-7 days warm)

**Why longer?**
- First reply might be "who are you?" (not interest)
- Second reply: "tell me more" (now interested)
- Third reply: "can you prove ROI?" (now questioning)
- 4th-5th reply: "okay, I'm ready" (now BANT complete)

**Implication:** Patience + follow-up discipline critical

### Conversion from Qualified to Sale: Similar (40-60%)

**Why:** Once they engage, they engage seriously. If they reply multiple times, they're genuinely interested.

---

## Daily Outreach Recommendation

**Current plan:** 1 new cold prospect per day (same as warm leads)

**Recommendation:** Consider 3-5 new cold prospects per day

**Why:**
- Cold reply rate is 3-8% (vs warm 15-25%)
- Need higher volume to fill pipeline
- 5 cold emails/day = ~0.25 replies (1 reply every 4 days)
- 1 cold email/day = ~0.05 replies (1 reply every 20 days)

**Conservative approach:** Start with 1/day, increase to 3/day after 2 weeks if replies are low

---

## Follow-up Sequence for Cold Leads

**Unlike warm leads, cold leads need 5-7 touchpoints:**

| Touch | Day | Action | Purpose |
|-------|-----|--------|---------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Email #01 (ROI + problem) | Grab attention |
| 2 | Day 3 | Email #02 (BANT discovery) | If reply → qualify |
| 3 | Day 7 | Case study + reference | If no reply → nurture |
| 4 | Day 14 | "Following up" + different angle | Persistence |
| 5 | Day 21 | Social proof (testimonial) | Build credibility |
| 6 | Day 28 | Limited-time offer (if available) | Create urgency |
| 7 | Day 35 | Final touch, then archive | Last chance to engage |

**Rule:** If reply at ANY point, move to BANT discovery + personalized follow-up (same as warm)

## Content Strategy for Cold Leads

### Lead with ROI, Not Features

❌ Wrong:
```
"Our MA1 laser welder is 4x faster than arc welding"
```

✅ Right:
```
"You can reduce welding labor costs from $180 to $25 per job"
```

### Use Social Proof Early

❌ Wrong:
```
"We're a leading supplier of laser welding equipment"
```

✅ Right:
```
"We just placed 12 MA1 units with shops in {{region}} in the last 6 months. Here are their references..."
```

### Lead with Specific Numbers

❌ Wrong:
```
"Most shops see savings with laser welding"
```

✅ Right:
```
"ABC Fabrication saved $48K/year in labor costs in the first 12 months. XYZ Manufacturing reduced weld time from 4 hours to 1 hour per job."
```

### Make It Easy to Say Yes

❌ Wrong:
```
"Would you be interested in learning more?"
```

✅ Right:
```
"Let me send you a 2-minute ROI calculator specific to your shop. What's your typical job volume per week?"
```

## Tracking Expectations

### Metrics to Monitor

- **Daily new cold outreach:** 1-5 (planned)
- **Reply rate:** 3-8% (not a failure if low)
- **BANT qualification rate:** 20-30% of replies (longer timeline)
- **Sales cycle:** 60-120 days (longer than warm)
- **Cost per qualified lead:** $300-500 (higher due to lower reply rate)

### Success Indicators

✅ Getting 5-10% reply rate after 2 weeks = good  
✅ Getting 1-2 BANT-qualified leads per month = on track  
✅ Getting first customer within 60-90 days = successful pipeline

❌ Getting 0-2% reply rate after 1 month = template/messaging issue  
❌ Getting 0 BANT-qualified in 30 days = process issue
