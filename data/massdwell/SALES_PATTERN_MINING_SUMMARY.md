## 🔥 TOP 5 INSIGHTS (Data-Backed)

### 1. **Site Visits = Conversion Catalyst** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Evidence:** 2 won deals both had site visits, 0 won without
- **Steve's Pattern:** "took carlos onsite... assessed feasibility... gave pricing on the spot"
- **Bot Change:** Replace "schedule a call" with "schedule a site visit"
- **Confidence:** 🔥 HIGH (100% correlation)

### 2. **Custom Design Answers Aesthetic Objections** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Evidence:** Nicole (WON) wanted "cottage look" → Steve customized → closed
- **Evidence:** Beth (LOST) wanted "different aesthetic" → bot accepted → lost
- **Steve's Pattern:** Immediately pivoted to custom design, sent lookbook
- **Bot Change:** Never accept aesthetic objection, always offer customization
- **Confidence:** 🔥 HIGH (proven win vs proven loss)

### 3. **Location Qualification Prevents Waste** ⭐⭐
- **Evidence:** 9 lost deals = out of state (CA, FL, HI, CT)
- **Problem:** Bot spent qualification time on non-serviceable leads
- **Bot Change:** Ask location FIRST, before any other qualification
- **Confidence:** 🔥 HIGH (9 wasted conversations)

### 4. **ROI Math Prevents Price Objections** ⭐⭐
- **Evidence:** Lost deals with rental intent balked at $270K but wanted $2,800/mo income
- **Math Gap:** They didn't see 8-year payback ($270K ÷ $33.6K/year)
- **Steve's Pattern:** Transparent breakdown "$150K ADU + $30K sitework"
- **Bot Change:** Lead with ROI calculator for rental income intent
- **Confidence:** 🟡 MEDIUM (pattern observed, needs A/B test)

### 5. **Site Complexity = Opportunity, Not Blocker** ⭐
- **Evidence:** Steve's detailed notes on setbacks, septic, wells
- **Pattern:** "Very tight lot, but we should have the room to do it"
- **Bot Change:** Engage with constraints, show domain knowledge
- **Confidence:** 🟡 MEDIUM (correlates with Steve's wins)

## 💡 LESSONS LEARNED (Direct from Data)

### ✅ What Works (Do More)
1. Site visits close deals - push hard for them
2. Custom design answers aesthetic objections
3. Specific references to their town/situation
4. Transparent cost breakdowns
5. Engaging with site complexity (not fleeing)

### ❌ What Doesn't Work (Stop Doing)
1. Accepting objections without 2 pivot attempts
2. Generic templates that could apply to anyone
3. Qualifying out-of-state leads
4. Waiting for price objection before showing ROI
5. Asking "would you like a call?" instead of specific times

### 🚨 What's Risky (Handle Carefully)
1. Multi-unit projects - escalate to Steve immediately
2. Emotional/family situations - personal touch required
3. High-value deals (>$200K) - Steve's expertise wins
4. Complex sites - engage but verify with feasibility study
