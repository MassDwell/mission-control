## 🚨 OBJECTION HANDLING

### "That's too expensive" / "Seems pricey"
> "I get it — this is a real investment. A few things to consider: our base prices include delivery and installation, which contractors don't. You're also getting 8–12 weeks, not 12–18 months of carrying costs. And in Greater Boston, you're looking at $2,000–$3,500/month in rent — that's a payback in under 5 years on most models. We also have financing options. What's your target number? Let's see if we can engineer something that works."

### "I'm not sure about the aesthetic / I want something more design-forward"
> "Our website shows a few basic examples — it's not the limit of what we can build. We have our own production facility and an in-house design team. We can match your home's style, go modern, go traditional — whatever you want. We're actually offering a free design consultation right now. Would you like to see some custom examples we've done?"

*(Never accept this objection. Always make one more offer.)*

### "How long does permitting take?"
> "We handle 100% of permitting — that's one of the biggest reasons people use us. Timing varies by town, but since the February 2025 state law change, most single-family homeowners in MA can get a building permit without a zoning board hearing. We're seeing 4–12 weeks depending on the municipality. We know the local building departments and we manage the whole process."

### "I'm not ready yet / maybe next year"
> "Totally fine — and honestly perfect timing to start planning. Most projects take 8–12 weeks from permit approval. If we start the design conversation now, you'll be ready to break ground the moment you want to move forward. Prices may also go up. Can we at least do a quick site assessment? It's free and it tells you exactly what's possible on your property."

### "I don't know if I can build an ADU here"
> "Great news — since February 2025, ADUs under 900 sqft are allowed by-right in single-family zoning districts statewide in Massachusetts. That means no zoning board hearing, no neighbor notification — just a building permit. The only variables are local setback requirements and design standards. What town are you in? We probably know your municipality's specifics."

### "Do you do custom designs?"
> "Absolutely. What you see on our website is a starting point. We have our own factory and design team — we can build to your specs. A lot of our best projects started with a customer saying 'I want something different.'"

### "What about utilities?"
> "It's all included. We connect to your existing water, sewer, and electric service. We handle the coordination with utility companies as part of our turnkey service. Off-grid options are available if that's relevant for your property."

### "I already talked to a contractor"
> "That's great — you're doing your research. One thing worth asking them: does their price include permitting? Does it include site prep and utility connections? With us, all of that is wrapped into our base price. We're also 8–12 weeks vs. the typical 12–18 months for a custom build. Happy to do a side-by-side comparison if that's useful."

## 🎯 CUSTOMER PROFILES (Who's Calling)

### 👴 Rental Income Seeker (Most Common)
Homeowner 45–65, high equity, suburban Greater Boston. Thinks of ADU as retirement income.
**Lead with:** Monthly rent potential, payback period, "we handle everything"
**Key worry:** "Is it really worth it?" → Show ROI math

### 👨‍👩‍👴 Multigenerational Family
Needs housing for aging parent or adult child, nearby but separate.
**Lead with:** Design quality, privacy features, "fits with your home's aesthetic"
**Key worry:** "Will it look weird on my property?" → Show custom examples

### 🏗️ Real Estate Investor / Developer
Wants to add density to existing lots, "buy + ADU" strategy.
**Lead with:** Speed, scalability, repeat customer pricing
**Key worry:** Timeline and cost certainty → emphasize 8–12 weeks and fixed pricing

### 🏡 Empty Nester
Older homeowner, house too big, wants passive income or space for a caregiver.
**Lead with:** Simplicity of the process, "we handle it all"
**Key worry:** "Is this too complicated for me?" → Emphasize turnkey
