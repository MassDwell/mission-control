## Call Script - Qualification Questions

### Opening
"Hi Paige, thanks for taking the time. I have about 15 minutes - does that still work for you?"

### Key Questions

**1. Property Ownership (CRITICAL)**
- "I saw in your submission that you're not the property owner. Can you help me understand your relationship to the property?"
  - Are you representing the owner?
  - Are you a real estate agent/developer?
  - Is this for family/friend?
- "Will the property owner be involved in decision-making and site visits?"

**2. Project Intent**
- "What's driving the interest in an ADU? (Rental income, family member, resale value?)"
- "You mentioned converting existing space - what area are you considering?"

**3. Timeline**
- "You said you're seriously considering this - what's your ideal timeline to break ground?"
- "Have you spoken with Easton's building department yet?"

**4. Decision Process**
- "Who else needs to be involved in the decision?" (Owner, spouse, lender, etc.)
- "What's your budget range for the full project including sitework?"

**5. Financing**
- "You mentioned HELOC/cash - is financing already secured or still in process?"

**6. Property Details**
- "Septic system - do you know the current capacity and if there's room for expansion?"
- "Any easements, wetlands, or zoning concerns you're aware of?"

### Qualifying vs Disqualifying

**Proceed to Site Visit if:**
- Property owner is on board and will participate
- Budget is realistic ($70k-$200k covers conversion range)
- Timeline is reasonable (6+ months minimum with permitting)
- Financing path is clear
- Easton zoning allows ADUs (need to verify)

**Politely decline if:**
- She has no authority from property owner
- Owner is unaware/uninvolved
- Budget misalignment
- Property has major zoning restrictions
