# OBJECTION HANDLING RULES - MassDwell

**CRITICAL: Never Accept These Objections**

## ❌ NEVER Accept These As Final

### 1. **"Different Aesthetic" / "More Design Forward"**
**WRONG:** "Good luck with your search!"  
**RIGHT:** "We can build anything you want - custom design is what we do!"

**TEMPLATE (Steve's Beth Email - PERFECT EXAMPLE):**
> "Hey [Name],
> 
> Our website just gives a few simple examples of some different ADU's. We have our own production facility and can build anything you want.
> 
> For example, we are building this for a client in [location]: [link to example]
> 
> You could work with our in-house design team to design anything you want. I hope you reconsider using us.
> 
> We are also currently offering a limited time free design consultation.
> 
> Thanks,
> Steve"

**Key Elements:**
- ✅ Website is just examples (catalog ≠ limits)
- ✅ Own production facility (can build anything)
- ✅ Show actual custom project example (proof!)
- ✅ In-house design team
- ✅ Free design consultation offer
- ✅ Personal, direct tone

---

### 2. **"Too Expensive"**
**WRONG:** "I understand, best of luck!"  
**RIGHT:** "Let's explore options - financing, phasing, or value-engineering."

**Script:**
> "I get it - this is a big investment. Let's see if we can make it work: we offer financing options, we can phase the build, or we can value-engineer to hit your budget. What's your target number?"

---

### 3. **"Not Sure About Timing"**
**WRONG:** "Reach out when you're ready!"  
**RIGHT:** "Let's plan now so you're ahead when you're ready."

**Script:**
> "Perfect timing to start planning! Most projects take 8-12 weeks from approval. If we start design work now, you'll be ahead when you're ready to move forward. Plus, prices may go up if you wait. Can we at least do a site evaluation?"

---

## ✅ Objections We DO Accept (After One Attempt)

1. **Out of Service Area** (we only do MA)
   - Offer resources, future expansion note, gracious exit

2. **Already Signed Contract** with another builder
   - Wish them well, leave door open for future

3. **Project Cancelled/No Longer Needed**
   - Gracious exit, keep in database for future

---

## 🎯 The Rule

**"Always make ONE MORE OFFER before accepting no."**

Exceptions:
- Out of service area
- Already under contract elsewhere
- Project cancelled

Everything else = another solution exists!
