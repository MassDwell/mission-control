# PART 1: PROGRAM OVERVIEW — Where We're Coming From

## The Big Picture

MassDwell is not just an ADU company. We are a **modular construction company**. Our factory can produce ADUs, single-family homes, multi-family buildings, mixed-use structures, and fully custom modular projects. The factory process is largely the same regardless of what we're building — the capability scales with the opportunity.

This matters because our go-to-market strategy is **omnichannel**. One of our most important channels is B2B: we want **General Contractors, builders, and developers** to work with us and offer our product to *their* clients. They bring the customer relationship. We bring the factory, the speed, and the expertise. Everyone wins.

## Who We're Targeting

- General Contractors with active residential client bases
- Custom home builders
- Residential and commercial developers doing infill, multi-family, or mixed-use
- Remodeling companies whose clients ask about ADUs or additions

## Why This Channel Matters

A single GC relationship is worth multiple deals per year. They already have the trust of homeowners and developers. By positioning MassDwell as their modular construction partner, we become embedded in their service offering — not a one-time vendor. Every project they touch is a potential MassDwell sale.

## The Program: MassDwell Pro

MassDwell Pro is our formal trade partner program. It has three tiers, starting with a simple no-commitment referral structure and scaling up to a full certified partner relationship with co-branding, volume pricing, and joint marketing.

---

## Program Tiers

### Tier 1 — Referred Partner
No commitment required. A partner submits a lead, and if it closes, they earn a flat **$5,000 referral fee per unit**. This is the entry point — low friction, high upside. Ideal for builders who want to test the relationship before committing.

### Tier 2 — Preferred Partner
For partners who commit to 2+ referrals per year. They get co-branded proposal materials, priority scheduling, a dedicated MassDwell contact, and enhanced referral terms on multi-unit deals.

### Tier 3 — Certified Partner
For active volume partners (5+ units/year). They get listed on massdwell.com as a certified partner, first access to new models and pricing, joint marketing opportunities, white-label options, and preferred pricing on multi-family and large-scale projects.

---

## How the Workflow Works (From the Partner's Side)

The partner's job is simple:

1. Identify a client with a modular construction need — ADU, home, multi-family, anything
2. Submit the lead to MassDwell via a dedicated form or contact
3. MassDwell handles all of it: design, permitting, factory production, delivery, installation
4. The partner earns their fee. Their client gets a finished building in 8–12 weeks.

The partner never has to manage the build. They look like a hero for bringing the solution, and MassDwell does the heavy lifting.

---

## What MassDwell Commits To

- 48-hour response on all partner-submitted leads
- Co-branded proposal and quote documents
- Permitting handled in all markets we serve
- 8–12 week delivery timeline (scales with project complexity)
- Dedicated partner contact for Tier 2 and above
- Quarterly partner briefings on new configurations, pricing, and market updates
- Future: Partner portal via MassDwell Hub

---

## How Partners Join

1. Complete a short application (company name, license number, market area, typical project type, annual volume)
2. 15-minute intro call with the MassDwell team
3. Sign a simple partner agreement
4. Get onboarded with co-branded materials and a dedicated contact
