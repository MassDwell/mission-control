# MassDwell Pro — Trade Partner Program
*Last updated: 2026-04-05*

---

## What MassDwell Actually Builds

MassDwell is a **modular construction company** — not just an ADU company. Our factory produces:

- Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs)
- Single-family homes
- Multi-family / apartment buildings
- Mixed-use structures
- Custom modular builds

The factory process is largely the same regardless of scale. What changes is the configuration, not the capability. This means a trade partner can bring us a backyard ADU *or* a 12-unit infill development — and we can deliver both.

## Program Tiers

### Tier 1 — Referred Partner
- No commitment required
- Submit a lead, earn a referral fee per closed deal
- **$5,000 per unit at contract signing**
- Good for: Builders who want to test the relationship

### Tier 2 — Preferred Partner
- 2+ referrals/year commitment (or intent)
- Co-branded proposal materials (their logo + MassDwell)
- Priority scheduling on projects
- Dedicated contact/rep at MassDwell
- Enhanced referral terms or margin on multi-unit deals

### Tier 3 — Certified Partner
- Active volume relationship (5+ units/year)
- Listed on MassDwell website as a certified partner
- First access to new models, configurations, and pricing
- Joint marketing opportunities
- White-label option (their brand on the customer-facing experience)
- Preferred pricing on multi-family and large-scale projects

---

## How the Workflow Works (Their Side is Simple)

1. Partner identifies an opportunity — ADU, single-family, multi-family, anything modular
2. Partner submits lead to MassDwell (simple form or dedicated contact)
3. MassDwell scopes the project and handles the full sales process (or partner stays involved — their choice)
4. MassDwell handles: design, permitting, factory production, delivery, installation
5. Partner earns referral fee at contract signing or project completion
6. Partner looks like the hero who brought a turnkey modular solution to their client
