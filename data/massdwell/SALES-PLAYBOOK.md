## 🎯 Sales Process

### 1. First Contact Response (Within 15 minutes)
- Acknowledge inquiry immediately
- Express enthusiasm
- Ask 3-4 qualifying questions
- Offer calendar link for consultation

### 2. Qualifying Questions (Priority Order)
1. **Property location** (Massachusetts only right now)
2. **Timeline** (how soon do they need this?)
3. **Budget range** (ensures we're in the ballpark)
4. **Use case** (rental income, family, home office, etc.)
5. **Zoning status** (have they checked if ADUs are allowed?)

### 3. Value Proposition (Use These Points)
- **Speed:** 50% faster than traditional construction
- **Quality:** Factory-controlled environment = consistent quality
- **Service:** We handle permitting (biggest headache solved)
- **ROI:** Increase property value + potential rental income
- **Sustainability:** Energy-efficient, eco-friendly materials

### 4. Objection Handling

**"That seems expensive"**
→ Compare to traditional construction costs + time saved + rental income potential
→ Break down cost per sqft ($300/sqft turnkey is competitive)
→ Mention property value increase

**"How long does permitting take?"**
→ We handle all permitting and approvals
→ Timeline varies by municipality (typically 4-12 weeks)
→ We have relationships with local building departments

**"I want to customize the design"**
→ All models are customizable
→ In-house architecture team
→ Can build custom designs beyond our standard models

**"What about utilities hookup?"**
→ Included in our turnkey service
→ Connect to existing water, sewer, electric
→ Can discuss off-grid options if needed

**"Do you offer financing?"**
→ We can connect you with financing partners
→ Many customers use home equity loans
→ Some qualify for ADU-specific loan products

## 🔥 Sales Cadence by Lead Temperature

### HOT LEAD PROTOCOL (Score 12+)

**Definition:** Lead created <30 days ago AND (in Negotiation/Feasibility OR stated urgency "within 30 days")

**Day 0 (Same Day):**
- Call attempt #1 (morning)
- Call attempt #2 (afternoon)  
- Text message with intro
- Email with company overview

**Day 1:**
- Call attempt #3
- Send property-specific info if available
- Add to high-priority follow-up list

**Day 2-3:**
- Schedule site visit if connected
- Send relevant case study
- Calendar invite for feasibility call

**Day 4-7:**
- Conduct site feasibility assessment
- Take photos/measurements
- Review zoning requirements

**Day 7-14:**
- Deliver proposal with 3 options
- Schedule proposal review call
- Address objections

**Day 14-21:**
- Negotiation and revisions
- Financing discussion
- Contract preparation

**Day 21-30:**
- Final terms agreement
- Deposit collection
- Contract signing

---

### WARM LEAD PROTOCOL (Score 8-11)

**Definition:** Lead 30-90 days old OR in Conversation/Follow-up stage OR recent engagement

**Week 1:**
- Call + voicemail
- Text introduction
- Email #1: Value proposition

**Week 2:**
- Follow-up call
- Email #2: Case study relevant to their situation

**Week 3:**
- Call attempt
- Email #3: FAQ/objection handler

**Week 4:**
- Final call attempt for month
- Email #4: "Are you still interested?"

**Monthly (ongoing):**
- Newsletter inclusion
- Special offer notification
- Market update

---

### COLD LEAD PROTOCOL (Score 4-7)

**Definition:** Lead >90 days old AND no recent engagement AND in Recap/Future stages

**Re-Engagement Sequence:**

**Email 1 (Day 0):** Check-In
**Email 2 (Day 7):** Case study relevant to their situation
**Email 3 (Day 14):** Value offer (free site feasibility assessment)
**Email 4 (Day 21):** Final attempt ("Should I close your file?")

After Day 21: If no response, move to long-term nurture (monthly newsletter only)
