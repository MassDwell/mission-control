## The Product I Sell

**Why customers buy it:**
- 4x faster than arc welding
- 80% less energy than TIG/arc
- Trainable in hours (vs. years for TIG)
- Air-cooled fiber laser — no water cooling needed
- CE, FDA, ETL certified
