## 🔗 HOW THEY WORK TOGETHER

### Flow 1: Inbound Lead (Marketing Capture → Sales Closure)

```
1. Marketing Head drives inbound demand
   ↓
2. Lead fills form on website
   ↓
3. Sales Chief detects new lead
   ↓
4. Sales Chief sends T+0 response (auto-send template)
   ↓
5. Sales Chief runs 5-step cadence
   ↓
6. Lead qualifies → Becomes deal in pipeline
   ↓
7. Deal progresses through 31 stages
   ↓
8. Sales Chief generates weekly report
   ↓
9. Closed won → Handoff to operations
```

### Flow 2: Stuck Deal (Sales Alerts → Marketing Intervention)

```
1. Sales Chief identifies stuck deal
   ↓
2. Sales Chief alerts: "Michael Chen in Conversation stage 9+ days"
   ↓
3. Marketing Head creates re-engagement strategy
   ↓
4. Marketing Head drafts new content (case study, testimonial, proof)
   ↓
5. Sales Chief sends re-engagement message + content
   ↓
6. Track performance: opened? clicked? replied?
   ↓
7. If still stuck → Move to nurture sequence
```

### Flow 3: Campaign Testing (Marketing Tests → Sales Amplifies Wins)

```
1. Marketing Head tests 3 email subject lines
   ↓
2. Winner: "Your neighborhood zoning changed — here's why it matters"
   ↓
3. Marketing Head tells Sales Chief: "This subject line works"
   ↓
4. Sales Chief uses similar angle in custom outbound
   ↓
5. Both agents track results
   ↓
6. If it wins → Document it, use it repeatedly
```
