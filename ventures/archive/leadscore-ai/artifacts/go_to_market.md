# LeadScore.ai — Go-To-Market Strategy

**Stage:** BUILD/BETA  
**Date:** 2026-03-05  
**Author:** Codesmith  

## GTM Overview

**Target:** Mid-market SaaS companies (50–500 employees) with active inbound sales motion  
**Channel:** Content + PLG (product-led growth) + outbound  
**Timeline:** Beta (Month 1) → Paid Launch (Month 3) → Growth (Month 6+)  

## Phase 1: Beta (Month 1)

**Goal:** 5–10 beta customers, validate AI accuracy, collect NPS  

**Tactics:**
1. Steve's network — direct outreach to 20 sales leaders (warm intros)
2. LinkedIn DMs — target SDR/AE managers at Series A/B SaaS companies
3. HubSpot Partner Slack — post about beta availability
4. ProductHunt "Ship" page — build waitlist

**Offer:** Free beta access (unlimited leads) in exchange for weekly feedback calls

## Phase 2: Paid Launch (Month 3)

**Goal:** $1,000+ MRR, 10+ paying customers  

**Tactics:**
1. Convert best beta customers to Starter tier ($99/mo)
2. ProductHunt launch (main launch)
3. Content: "How we qualified 1,000 leads with AI" (case study)
4. Cold email campaign (Apollo.io) targeting VP Sales @ SaaS

## Phase 3: Growth (Month 6+)

**Goal:** $5,000+ MRR, 30+ paying customers  

**Tactics:**
1. SEO — target "lead qualification software", "AI lead scoring"
2. Partner channel — HubSpot + Salesforce App Marketplace
3. Referral program — 1 month free for each referral
4. Upsell beta customers from Starter → Growth

## Key Metrics (GTM)

| Month | Goal | Metric |
|-------|------|--------|
| 1 | Beta validation | 5 active beta customers, NPS ≥7 |
| 3 | Revenue | $1K MRR, 10 paying |
| 6 | Scale | $5K MRR, 30 paying |
| 12 | Growth | $15K MRR, 100 paying |
