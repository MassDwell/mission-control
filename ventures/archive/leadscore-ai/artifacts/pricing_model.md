# LeadScore.ai — Pricing Model

**Stage:** PRD/BUILD  
**Date:** 2026-03-05  
**Author:** Codesmith  

## Pricing Tiers

### Starter — $99/month
- 500 leads/month included
- $0.20 per lead overage
- API access
- Basic dashboard
- Email support

### Growth — $299/month
- 2,500 leads/month included
- $0.12 per lead overage
- API access + webhooks
- Full dashboard + analytics
- CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- Priority support

### Enterprise — $999/month
- 15,000 leads/month included
- $0.07 per lead overage
- Everything in Growth
- Custom AI model training
- SSO + advanced security
- Dedicated support + SLA

## Unit Economics (Target)

| Metric | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
|--------|---------|--------|-----------|
| ARPU | $99 | $299 | $999 |
| CAC target | <$200 | <$500 | <$2,000 |
| LTV (24mo) | $2,376 | $7,176 | $23,976 |
| LTV:CAC | 12x | 14x | 12x |

## Revenue Milestones

- Month 1: $0 (beta, free)
- Month 3: $1,000+ MRR (10 paying customers @ $99)
- Month 6: $5,000+ MRR (mix of tiers)
- Month 12: $15,000+ MRR (growth + enterprise mix)
