# LeadScore.ai — Idea

**Stage:** IDEA  
**Date:** 2026-03-05  
**Author:** Moonshot  

## Problem Statement

Sales teams waste 40–60% of their time reviewing low-quality leads. Manual lead qualification is slow, inconsistent, and error-prone — costing companies thousands in wasted sales cycles.

**Specific pain:** A sales rep reviews 100 inbound leads/week. 30–45 minutes spent per 100 leads on initial qualification. 60–70% are unqualified, yet every lead gets manual review time.

## Customer Persona

**Primary:** Sales Development Representatives (SDRs) at mid-market SaaS companies (50–500 employees)
- Receives 50–200 inbound leads/month
- Manually qualifies leads via email/form review
- Reports to VP Sales or Director of Revenue
- Pain: spending 2–5 hours/week on leads that never convert

**Secondary:** VP Sales / Revenue Operations
- Needs consistent, scalable qualification criteria
- Wants data on lead quality over time
- Budget: $500–$5K/month for tools that save sales time

## Value Proposition

AI analyzes leads in <5 seconds → Sales rep gets score (0–100), summary, and recommended routing (hot/warm/cold). Saves 2–5 hours/week per rep.
