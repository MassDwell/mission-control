## Planned Experiments

### Experiment 1: AI Scoring Accuracy
- **Hypothesis:** AI can classify leads with ≥85% accuracy vs. human review
- **Method:** 100 leads scored by AI vs. human expert (blind comparison)
- **Success criteria:** ≥85% agreement between AI and human
- **Timeline:** Week 1-2 of beta

### Experiment 2: Activation Rate
- **Hypothesis:** ≥50% of beta customers will submit 10+ leads in first week
- **Method:** Track API submissions per customer in first 7 days
- **Success criteria:** 5 of 10 beta customers submit ≥10 leads
- **Kill condition:** <10% activation after 2 weeks → KILL

### Experiment 3: NPS Validation
- **Hypothesis:** Customers rate AI scores ≥7/10 accuracy
- **Method:** Post-score rating prompt ("Was this score helpful?")
- **Success criteria:** Average rating ≥7.0 across 50+ ratings
- **Timeline:** Week 2-3 of beta
