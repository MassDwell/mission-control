## MassDwell Leads: WARM/HOT (Inbound)

**Source:** Self-directed (owned channels)
- Website visits (massdwell.com)
- Meta ads (Facebook/Instagram)
- Google ads (Search/Display)

**Characteristics:**
- ✅ Already aware of MassDwell
- ✅ Already interested in ADUs (or similar)
- ✅ Self-selected to visit/click
- ✅ Higher intent to engage

**Implications for Email:**
- Can be more assumptive ("I noticed you visited our site...")
- Can move faster through BANT qualification
- Higher expected reply rate
- Shorter nurture cycle (3-7 days to qualification)
- Can use "softer touch" consultative approach (they already want to hear from us)

**Current Volume:**
- ~589 callable leads across all stages
- Coming from Kommo CRM (email captures from web/ads)
- Existing pipeline: Initial Contact → Follow-Up → Negotiation

## Alpine Leads: COLD (Outbound)

**Source:** Database acquisition (external)
- Imported from fabrication/welding shop database
- NO prior engagement with Atlantic Laser
- NO prior awareness

**Characteristics:**
- ❌ Don't know Atlantic Laser
- ❌ Unsolicited contact
- ❌ Unknown if interested in laser welding
- ❌ Lower initial intent

**Implications for Email:**
- MUST lead with value prop (why should they listen?)
- MUST establish relevance upfront (we serve shops like yours)
- MUST expect lower reply rates (30% is good for cold)
- Longer nurture cycle needed (7-14 days to first reply, 21-30 days to qualification)
- Need "warmer" touches: case studies, proof, testimonials, ROI data
- More aggressive follow-up sequence (3-5 touchpoints before moving on)

**Current Volume:**
- ~3,000+ fabrication/welding shops in database
- Starting from zero engagement
- Building pipeline from scratch

## Email Template Adjustments Needed

### MassDwell (Warm) — Current Approach ✅
```
"I noticed your property..." (assumptive, they're in our funnel)
"Most homeowners think about ADUs for..." (problem agnostic)
"Open to a quick conversation?" (soft ask)
```
✅ This is correct for warm leads.

### Alpine (Cold) — NEEDS STRONGER VALUE PROP ⚠️

**Current approach:**
```
"4x faster welding..." (feature, not benefit)
"Shops in {{region}} looking to cut costs..." (generic)
```

**Recommended for cold:**
```
"We help fabrication shops in {{region}} cut welding time by 75%..."  (specific benefit)
"While reducing labor costs and improving quality consistency..."  (multiple benefits)
"This is how [Shop X] reduced their labor costs from $X to $X..."  (social proof)
"Are you open to a 10-minute conversation about ROI?" (direct ask with reason)
```

## Strategy Implications

### MassDwell
- **Goal:** Move fast, capture intent, schedule quickly
- **Tone:** Consultative, assumptive (they chose us)
- **Cadence:** 1 email/day, fast replies (same day follow-up if interested)
- **Success:** High conversion rate from qualified to closed

### Alpine
- **Goal:** Build credibility, prove ROI, overcome skepticism
- **Tone:** Educational, benefit-driven, proof-heavy
- **Cadence:** 1 email/day but expect 10-14 day first reply lag
- **Success:** High volume of outreach + longer nurture = eventual conversions

## Standing Rule

**MassDwell & Alpine use different strategies because they're fundamentally different lead sources.**

Don't apply warm-lead tactics to cold leads (will fail). Don't go too slow with warm leads (will lose them).
