## 11:24 AM — Sales Bot "Just Researching" Rule

**Implemented critical sales workflow rule:**

**Problem:** When prospects reply saying they're "just researching" or "not ready yet", sales bot was continuing to send follow-ups and annoying them.

**Solution:** Automatic detection and pipeline management
1. Detect "researching" signals in replies:
   - "just researching", "not doing anything right now"
   - "maybe in the future", "not ready yet"
   - "just browsing", "exploring options"
2. Move lead to **"Future Contact"** stage (status_id: 66451842)
3. **Stop all automated follow-ups** (sales bot skips these leads)
4. Log response as note in Kommo

**Files Updated:**
- `scripts/sales_bot_reply_monitor.py` — Added researching detection + move_to_future_contact()
- `scripts/sales_bot_auto_engage.py` — Added Future Contact to skip_stages list
- `data/massdwell/SALES-BOT-FUTURE-CONTACT.md` — Documentation

**Tested:** ✅ All detection working correctly
- "Just researching" → researching
- "Not doing anything right now" → researching  
- "Very interested" → positive

**Impact:** Prevents annoying unready prospects, keeps them warm for future re-engagement

**UPDATE 11:30 AM:** Added auto-advance to "Conversation Started"
- When ANY lead replies (not "researching"), move to "Conversation Started" (86738635)
- Only advances from cold stages (Incoming, Welcome, Follow-ups, Recap)
- Skips leads already in advanced stages (Site Feasibility, Negotiation)
- Sales bot stops emailing once in "Conversation Started" → Manual follow-up by Nick/Steve

**Pipeline Flow:**
1. New lead → Cold stages (bot emails)

3. "Just researching" reply → "Future Contact" (pause for 3-6 months)
