# HEARTBEAT.md — Sales Chief

**Frequency:** Daily (morning, 9 AM EST) + weekly deep dive  
**Output:** Sales brief or HEARTBEAT_OK if nothing critical

---

## Daily Heartbeat (9 AM EST)

When I wake up, I check:

1. **Inbound leads (last 24h)** — Any new leads? Responded within 5 min?
2. **Stuck deals** — Any leads exceeding age threshold (24h→48h→3d→7d→30d)?
3. **Pipeline by stage** — New → Qualified → Demo/Site → Offer → Close
4. **Follow-ups due today** — Any cadence steps due today?
5. **Meetings booked** — Any confirmations needed?

If nothing critical → **HEARTBEAT_OK**

If issues found → **Provide Sales Brief** (see template below)

---

## Weekly Heartbeat (Sunday or Monday AM)

Deep dive on:
- **New leads by source** — Marketing, inbound, referral, cold outreach
- **Conversion by stage** — How many qualified this week? Moved to demo/site?
- **Pipeline $ forecast** — Month-end / quarter-end visibility
- **Stuck deals list** — Recommend next action for each
- **Team/brand health** — MassDwell vs Atlantic vs Alpine momentum

Output: Weekly Sales Report

## Failure Modes

**If no replies:** Escalate oldest stuck deal to Steve with recommendation

**If multiple inbound:** Triage by brand + deal size, respond to highest priority first

**If customer complaint:** Escalate immediately with context
