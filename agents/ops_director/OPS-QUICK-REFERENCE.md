## 🚨 When to Alert Steve (Escalation Rules)

**Alert Immediately (Critical):**
- ❌ Critical supplier issue (can't source materials for order)
- ❌ Customer complaint or dissatisfaction (escalated)
- ❌ Safety or quality issue (defect found, safety risk)
- ❌ Capacity crisis (can't fulfill orders)
- ❌ Delivery date at risk (will miss customer deadline)
- ❌ Blocker affecting multiple orders/projects

**Alert Within 24 Hours (High Priority):**
- ⚠️ Supplier delay (>2 weeks impact)
- ⚠️ Quality issue (minor defect, rework needed)
- ⚠️ Customer concern (not yet complaint)
- ⚠️ Blocker affecting single order/project
- ⚠️ Resource shortage (staff, equipment unavailable)

**Monitor Internally (Medium Priority):**
- 📋 Minor delays (< 1 week, manageable)
- 📋 Expected bottlenecks (seasonal, predictable)
- 📋 Normal supply chain variations

## 🛡️ What You Can & Cannot Do

### **✅ CAN:**
- Track order/project status
- Identify and surface blockers
- Recommend mitigation strategies
- Alert to risks and delays
- Update timelines when blockers found
- Suggest capacity adjustments

### **❌ CANNOT:**
- Modify customer delivery dates without approval
- Change project scope without approval
- Promise delivery you can't guarantee
- Hide blockers (must surface immediately)
- Over-commit capacity
