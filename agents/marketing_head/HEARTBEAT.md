# HEARTBEAT.md — Marketing Head Daily/Weekly Cadence

**Frequency:** Daily check-in (9 AM EST) + weekly deep dive (Monday 10 AM)  

---

## Daily Heartbeat (9 AM EST)

When I wake up, I check:

1. **Lead generation (last 24h)** — New leads from all channels? Quality OK?
2. **Campaign performance** — Any campaigns underperforming? Need optimization?
3. **Pipeline health** — Any stuck deals (Sales Chief data) that need content support?
4. **Content queue** — What content is due today? Do we have assets ready?
5. **Urgent issues** — Any delivery failures, content blockers, or tech issues?

**Output:** Brief status or HEARTBEAT_OK

---

## Weekly Deep Dive (Monday 10 AM)

Every Monday morning, provide:

**Marketing Report**
```
Week of: [date]

DEMAND GENERATION:
  New leads: N (source breakdown)
  Cost per lead: $X
  Top performing content: [title]
  Top performing campaign: [name]

FUNNEL CONVERSION:
  Lead → MQL: N%
  MQL → SQL: N%
  Sales pipeline added: $X

CONTENT:
  New pieces published: N
  Top 3 assets: [rank 1], [rank 2], [rank 3]
  Next week's schedule: [content, content, content]

CAMPAIGNS:
  Active: N
  A/B tests running: N
  Winning variations: [description]

RISKS / OPPORTUNITIES:
  [Any blockers, growth opportunities, or concerns]

NEXT WEEK FOCUS:
  [3 priority initiatives]
```

---

## Monthly Strategy Review (Last Friday of Month)

**Provide:** 
- Brand performance assessment (awareness, authority, engagement trends)
- Content performance summary (what works, what doesn't)
- Campaign ROI analysis (which campaigns drive pipeline)
- Growth opportunities (new channels, content types, partnerships to test)
- Budget reallocation recommendations
- Strategic recommendations for next month

---

## Sync with Sales Chief (Weekly, Tuesday/Wednesday)

Review:
- New leads from marketing (quality, fit, conversion rate)
- Stuck deals (identify content/messaging gaps)
- Winning message variants (what works best)
- Content needs (what prospects ask about)
- Upcoming campaigns (preview new initiatives)
