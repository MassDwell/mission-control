## 🎬 Real Campaign Example: MassDwell "ADU Zoning Made Easy"

### Campaign Brief

**Brand:** massdwell  
**Goal:** Generate 40+ leads from homeowners confused about zoning  
**Target:** Greater Boston homeowners, ages 40-65, researching ADU feasibility  
**Channels:** Google Ads (search), Facebook (interest targeting), Email nurture  
**Budget:** $1,500/month  
**Timeline:** 4 weeks  

### Content Assets

1. **Landing Page:** "Is Your Lot ADU-Ready?"
   - Zoning quiz (5 questions)
   - Lead capture form
   - Instant feedback: "Your lot is viable" or "Here's what to check"

2. **Blog Post:** "ADU Zoning in Massachusetts (2026 Update)"
   - SEO-optimized (target: "ADU zoning Massachusetts")
   - 1,500 words, covers all questions homeowners ask
   - Link to zoning quiz

3. **Facebook Ads** (3 variations)
   - "Confused about ADU zoning? We have the answers"
   - Video testimonial from recent customer
   - Before/after property photos

4. **Email Sequence** (3 parts)
   - Email 1: "Your zoning results + next steps"
   - Email 2: "5 financing options your lender should tell you about"
   - Email 3: "Ready to explore? Here's what a site visit looks like"

5. **Lead Qualification Form**
   - Town
   - Property type
   - Goal (rental, family, ADU)
   - Timeline
   - Budget comfort

### Performance Expectations

- **Leads:** 40+
- **Cost per lead:** $35-50 (1,500 / 40)
- **MQL conversion:** 30%+ (12+ MQL from 40 leads)
- **SQL conversion:** 25%+ (3+ SQL from 12 MQL)

### Timeline

- **Week 1:** Design landing page, write blog, create ad creative
- **Week 2:** Launch blog + Facebook ads, set up email sequence
- **Week 3:** Monitor performance, optimize (adjust bids, test creative)
- **Week 4:** Final report, recommendations for scaling
