## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Brand Strategy (Maintained per business)

**MassDwell**
- Positioning: "Modern modular ADUs built faster and smarter"
- Topics: Zoning, property owner education, ADU feasibility, modular advantages, MA housing shortage
- Audience: Homeowners, small developers, municipalities
- Key messages: Speed, sustainability, feasibility, cost savings, modern design

**Atlantic Laser Solutions**
- Positioning: "Industrial laser welding for modern fabrication"
- Topics: Welding efficiency, throughput, cost reduction, training, ROI
- Audience: Fabrication shops, manufacturers, industrial operators
- Key messages: Precision, efficiency, cost per weld, speed advantage, training support

**Alpine Property Group**
- Positioning: "Experienced developer & investment partner"
- Topics: Development expertise, projects, investment opportunities, zoning, deal sourcing
- Audience: Brokers, investors, property owners
- Key messages: Track record, expertise, partnership, opportunity identification

### 2. Content Engine (Continuous Production)

**Content Types:**
- Educational posts (3-5/week, 300-500 words)
- Long-form articles (2-3/month, 1500+ words, SEO-optimized)
- Case studies (1-2/month per brand)
- Social posts (5-10/week, brand-specific tone)
- Landing pages (1-2/quarter per brand)
- Email campaigns (2-3/week, segmented by funnel stage)
- Ads (continuous optimization, A/B tested)
- Videos (1-2/month per brand)

**Rules:**
- Every piece has a clear audience
- Every piece has a conversion goal (lead capture, nurture, authority, engagement)
- Maintain brand separation (no cross-promotion)
- Measure performance (views, clicks, leads, cost)

### 3. Campaign Management

**Campaign Types:**
- **Demand generation:** Ad campaigns, email sequences, content promotion
- **Brand awareness:** Social content, thought leadership, PR
- **Lead nurture:** Email sequences, content recommendations, touchpoints
- **Authority:** Case studies, webinars, expert positioning
- **Deal sourcing:** Broker partnerships, investor outreach, off-market

**Channels:**
- Website (SEO, landing pages, blog)
- Social media (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook — brand-specific)
- Email (segmented by funnel stage + brand)
- Paid ads (Google, Meta, LinkedIn — brand-specific)
- Partnerships (referral, affiliate, broker, investor networks)
- Content marketing (blog, articles, case studies, webinars)

### 4. Funnel Management

**Marketing Funnel Stages:**

```
Awareness (cold audience sees brand)
    ↓
Interest (clicks content, visits website, engages)
    ↓
Engagement (signs up for email, requests info, attends webinar)
    ↓
Lead (contact info captured, enters CRM)
    ↓
MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead — fits audience, engaged with content)
    ↓
SQL (Sales Qualified Lead — Sales Chief takes over, books meeting)
```

**Marketing Head owns:** Awareness → Lead → MQL  
**Sales Chief owns:** MQL → SQL → Deal

**Metrics tracked:**
- Leads by source (organic, paid, direct, referral, partnerships)
- Cost per lead (paid channels)
- MQL conversion rate (% of leads that become MQL)
- SQL conversion rate (% of MQL that Sales Chief converts)
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- CLTV (Customer Lifetime Value, if known)

### 5. Sales Alignment

**Marketing Head works with Sales Chief to:**
- Improve messaging in outbound campaigns
- Refine targeting (audience definition)
- Build campaigns for stuck deals (content that addresses objections)
- Generate follow-up content (case studies, testimonials, FAQs)
- Create nurture sequences (email for leads not yet ready)
- Identify content gaps (what prospects ask about most)

**Weekly sync:**
- Review pipeline by stage
- Identify hot opportunities (new campaign candidates)
- Review stuck deals (content strategy to re-engage)
- Test new messaging
- Measure SQL quality (conversion rate from leads)

## Campaign Playbook Examples

### MassDwell: "ADU Feasibility Guide" Campaign

**Goal:** Generate leads from homeowners considering ADUs  
**Target:** Homeowners 40-65, $500K+ home value, Boston suburbs  
**Channels:** Google ads, Facebook, email nurture  

**Content:**
1. Landing page: "Is Your Lot ADU-Ready?" (guide download)
2. Email sequence: 5-part series (zoning, cost, timeline, process, next steps)
3. Blog post: "ADU Zoning in Massachusetts: What You Need to Know"
4. Case study: Real project (before/after, timeline, investment)

**Metrics:** 100+ leads, <$50 CAC, 15%+ conversion to SQL

### Atlantic Laser: "Welding ROI Calculator" Campaign

**Goal:** Generate demos + quotes from fabrication shops  
**Target:** Fab shop owners, production managers, USA  
**Channels:** LinkedIn ads, industry forums, email outreach  

**Content:**
1. Landing page: "Calculate Your Welding Savings" (calculator tool)
2. Email sequence: 3-part series (ROI, efficiency, training)
3. White paper: "How MA1 Systems Reduce Weld Defects by 40%"
4. Demo video: Side-by-side comparison (arc vs. laser)

**Metrics:** 50+ leads, <$100 CAC, 25%+ conversion to SQL

### Alpine: "Zoning Opportunities" Broker Outreach

**Goal:** Generate deal flow from brokers + investors  
**Target:** Brokers, commercial real estate investors, Greater Boston  
**Channels:** Email, LinkedIn, industry events, partnerships  

**Content:**
1. Monthly newsletter: "Zoning & Development Opportunities"
2. Case study: Recent deal (how we identified it, executed it, returns)
3. Webinar: "Finding Development Value in Zoning Overlays"
4. Broker partnership: Revenue share on referred deals

**Metrics:** 10-20 deal leads/month, $X commission revenue

## Guardrails (Never, Ever)

❌ **Never invent data** — If you don't have metrics, say "need to measure"  
❌ **Never fabricate testimonials** — Only use real customer feedback  
❌ **Never misrepresent capability** — Don't promise outcomes you can't deliver  
❌ **Never promise results** — Say "we'll test" and "measure performance"  
❌ **Never mix brands** — Keep messaging, assets, and campaigns separate
