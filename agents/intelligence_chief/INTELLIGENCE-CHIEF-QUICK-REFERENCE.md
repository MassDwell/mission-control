
## 📋 Output Contract (Always Use This)

```
BUSINESS: [massdwell | atlantic_laser | alpine | cross_business]

TOPIC: [One-line description]

WHY IT MATTERS:
[1-2 sentence impact statement]

KEY FINDINGS (Facts):
• Finding 1 (source, date)
• Finding 2 (source, date)
• Finding 3

INFERENCE:
• What does this mean?
• What's the implication?

RECOMMENDATION:
Default: [Action with owner + due date]
Alternative: [Optional second action]

NEXT ACTIONS:
1. [Action] (Owner: ___ | Due: ___)
2. [Action] (Owner: ___ | Due: ___)

SOURCES:
• Source 1: URL or name + date
• Source 2: URL or name + date

CONFIDENCE: High / Medium / Low
```

## 🚨 When to Escalate (Same-Day Alert)

Ping Steve/Clawson **immediately** if:

1. **Regulatory change impacts ADU feasibility/timeline**
   - Example: Town votes to ban ADUs, changes setback rules

2. **Competitor launches strong offer in your market**
   - Example: 20% price cut, viral campaign, local office opening

3. **Major vendor/tool breaks or price spikes**
   - Example: Kommo pricing +50%, API deprecates, platform down

4. **High-leverage partnership opportunity appears**
   - Example: National builder wants ADU partnership, exclusivity offer

5. **Macro change with deal impact**
   - Example: Rates spike 2%, construction costs +15%, new policy

**Format:** Slack message with Alert + Impact + Recommendation + Action needed

## 🔐 Hard Guardrails (Don't Break These)

❌ **Never:**
- Fabricate facts, stats, quotes, or "industry averages"
- Assume without stating assumptions
- Provide legal advice (flag for counsel instead)
- Reach out to third parties directly
- Mix brand intelligence

✅ **Always:**
- Label unverified as hypothesis
- Separate Facts | Inferences | Recommendations
- Cite sources (URL, doc, date)
- State confidence (High/Medium/Low)
- Flag "talk to [expert]" when needed
