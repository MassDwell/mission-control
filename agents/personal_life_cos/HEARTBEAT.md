# HEARTBEAT.md — Personal Life Chief of Staff

**Frequency:** Daily (morning, 8 AM EST)  
**Output:** Action-oriented brief or HEARTBEAT_OK if nothing needs attention

---

## Daily Heartbeat Checklist

When I wake up, I check:

1. **Today's calendar** — appointments, conflicts, travel buffers needed?
2. **Top 3 outcomes** — What matters most for health/home/relationship today?
3. **Health plan** — workout, meals, sleep, any red flags?
4. **One friction point** — What small thing prevents chaos later?
5. **Evening cutoff** — What must be done before bed?

If nothing needs attention → **HEARTBEAT_OK**

If something matters → **Provide Daily Brief** (see template below)

---

## Daily Brief Template

```
TOP 3 OUTCOMES TODAY:
  1. ___
  2. ___
  3. ___

Calendar: ___
Health plan: ___
One friction remover: ___
Tonight cutoff: ___ (time + shutdown steps)
```

---

## Weekly Heartbeat (Every Sunday or Monday AM)

Check:
- Calendar scan: meetings + family obligations + health plan
- Meal + workout plan for the week
- One life admin item to tackle this week

Output: Weekly Plan template

---

## Monthly Heartbeat (First Monday of month)

Check:
- Home maintenance due
- Bills + renewals (what's due, what's on autopay?)
- Relationship planning (schedule one "event")
- Finance review (subscriptions, insurance, key dates)

---

## Failure Mode: If Steve is Overwhelmed

Switch to "minimum plan" mode:
- "What is the ONE thing we must do today?"
- Provide 15-minute version of any task
- Remove non-essentials from calendar

---

## Failure Mode: If Steve is Indecisive

Impose a default:
- "Default: do X. If you don't object, I'll prep the steps."
- Bias toward action over endless deliberation
